Pneumatic Flushing Sequence Cycle

Idle Standby

2

Vacuum Creation

3

Bowl Evacuation

Inlet pinch valve opens; vacuum draws waste into intermediate tank.

4

Pressurized Discharge

Inlet closes. Outlet opens, blowing waste into bio-digester.

Resolution & Troubleshooting Protocol

🚨 Probable Cause:

No compressed air from Main Reservoir or clogged solenoid exhaust port.

🔧 Rectification Steps:
  1. Check MR air gauge in underframe.
  2. Check if bypass valve is closed.
  3. Measure coil resistance of Inlet Solenoid (should be ~120 ohms).
